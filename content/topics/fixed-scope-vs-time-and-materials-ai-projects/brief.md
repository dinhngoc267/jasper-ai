# Post Brief

**Title**: Fixed-Scope vs. Time-and-Materials for AI Projects — Why the Wrong Choice Costs You Either Way

**Target keyword**: fixed price vs time and materials AI project

**Audience**: Founders and technical leads at small-to-mid-size companies (2–250 people) who are comparing contract structures across vendor proposals — one quotes fixed-price, another quotes T&M/hourly, and they don't know which protects them. Bottom-funnel, vendor-selection/procurement stage.

**Angle**: The fixed-price vs. T&M debate is usually framed as a risk-transfer question (who eats the overage), but for AI projects specifically, both models fail for the same underlying reason if they skip architecture validation first: fixed-price without discovery means the vendor prices in a big risk buffer (you overpay) or under-prices and cuts corners when reality hits (you get a worse product); T&M without discovery means open-ended hours with no way to know if you're funding real progress or an architecture dead end. The actual fix isn't picking a contract type — it's sequencing: a short, fixed-price (or capped) discovery phase to resolve architecture risk, THEN a fixed-scope build priced with real information. This is a direct expression of how Jasper AI structures engagements.

**Supporting points**:
- Explain fixed-price plainly: predictable cost, but the vendor must price in uncertainty — for AI, that uncertainty is often architecture fit, which is hard to price honestly upfront, so vendors either inflate the quote or under-scope and cut corners later.
- Explain T&M plainly: flexible and can be fair, but for a non-technical buyer it's hard to tell if hours are buying progress or exploration — and AI discovery genuinely does involve exploration, which makes T&M feel riskier than it needs to.
- The actual failure mode isn't the contract type, it's applying either one to the WHOLE project instead of splitting by risk profile: discovery (inherently uncertain, should be capped/fixed and short) vs. build (should be genuinely fixed-scope once discovery resolves the big unknown).
- Give the reader a simple test: ask any vendor "which of these two phases is this quote for?" — if they can't separate discovery from build, the contract type barely matters because the risk hasn't actually been isolated.
- Reinforce credibility: this is exactly the structure Jasper AI uses — paid discovery (use-case evaluation + architecture design) before any fixed-scope build is quoted.
